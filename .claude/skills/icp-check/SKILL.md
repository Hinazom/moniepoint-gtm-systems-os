# ICP Check

Use this skill when evaluating whether a company is a suitable prospect for Moniepoint Business Banking and Payments.

## Instructions

1. Read `icp/icp.md` and `icp/signals.md` before evaluating the company.

2. Research the company using available sources. Investigate:
   - Industry and business model.
   - Headquarters and operating locations.
   - Number of physical branches or outlets, where available.
   - Business size and operational complexity.
   - Payment collection and financial operations, where evidence is available.
   - Recent expansion, recruitment and other relevant developments.

3. Evaluate ICP fit independently of buying signals. Do not assume that the absence of a detectable signal means the company is a poor ICP fit.

4. Score ICP fit from 1 to 10 using the following considerations:
   - Industry and business model alignment.
   - Physical footprint and transaction characteristics.
   - Operational and financial complexity.
   - Potential relevance of Moniepoint's business banking and payment services.

5. Evaluate buying signals separately. Identify:
   - Signal type.
   - Supporting evidence.
   - Source and publication date.
   - Recency.
   - Relevance to Moniepoint's potential offering.
   - Confidence level.

6. Distinguish verified facts, reasonable inferences and unverified hypotheses. Do not present recruitment or expansion as proof that a company intends to purchase or replace financial software.

7. Assign one qualification outcome:
   - Qualified: sufficient evidence of ICP alignment.
   - Disqualified: clear evidence that the company falls outside the target profile.
   - Further research required: insufficient evidence to make a reliable decision.

8. Explain the decision and identify missing information that could change the assessment.

## Output

Return the following:

- Company overview.
- ICP fit score (1–10) with supporting reasons.
- Buying signals detected, with sources and dates.
- Evidence quality and confidence.
- Qualification outcome.
- Key information gaps.
- Recommended next action.

Never invent company information, branch counts, financial figures, buying signals or sources. Preserve uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
