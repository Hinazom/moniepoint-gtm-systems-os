# Moniepoint GTM Systems OS

An evidence-led GTM Engineering project exploring how business intelligence, ICP research, buying signals and structured workflows can support customer acquisition for Moniepoint Business Banking and Payments in Nigeria.

**Market:** Nigeria  
**Focus:** Retail chains, FMCG distributors and multi-location businesses  
**Status:** Research automation and systems design foundation

## Project Overview

The objective is to develop a structured system for identifying, researching, qualifying and prioritising businesses with potential banking and payment needs.

The project connects:

- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) research
- Buying-signal identification
- Company enrichment and account qualification
- Account prioritisation
- Decision-maker research
- Prospect preparation and human review

## Current Implementation

Python scripts automate public-source discovery, webpage extraction, source registration and Gemini-assisted research synthesis.

The architecture and account qualification workflow are documented. Clay integrations, automated account-level scoring, CRM integration and outbound activation remain proposed, not implemented.

## Design Principles

- Evidence-backed research and traceable sources.
- Separate ICP fit from buying intent.
- Distinguish facts, inferences and hypotheses.
- Account for false positives in buying signals.
- Require human approval before external sales engagement.

## Objective

Build a repeatable GTM intelligence system that turns public business information into structured account research, qualification and sales-ready prospect preparation.
