### 2. Segment-Specific ICP Definitions

#### A. FMCG Distributors & Wholesalers

- **Workflow:** High volume B2B collections; frequent supplier payments; high reliance on trade credit; complex inventory turnover [S029, S030, S031].
- **Mandatory Qualification:** Operates multiple depots or significant warehousing; maintains trade receivables/credit-based sales model.
- **Economic Buyer:** Owner/Founder or Managing Director (for mid-size); Finance Controller/Treasury Manager (for large).
- **Buying Signals:** Recruiting for finance roles [S029]; expansion into new geographic regions/depots [S034]; new supplier distribution agreements [S028].

#### B. Petrol Station Networks

- **Workflow:** High-frequency, low-ticket B2C; mixed payments (cash, POS, transfer); extreme sensitivity to T+1 settlement delays [S035, S038].
- **Mandatory Qualification:** Owns or manages 3+ stations; performs daily cash/POS reconciliation.
- **Economic Buyer:** Owner/Managing Director (often hands-on for small/mid networks); Operations Director.
- **Buying Signals:** Infrastructure expansion (new stations); adoption of CNG/EV or diversified retail offerings [S036].

---

### 3. Campaign-Specific ICP Recommendation

The existing focus on **RETAIL_SUPERMARKET** and **FMCG_DISTRIBUTOR** is **commercially justified** but **too narrow**.

**Recommendation:** Expand the campaign to include **PETROL_STATION_NETWORKS**.

- **Reasoning:** The evidence confirms a massive shift to digital payments in this sector (43% of fuel sales) [S035, S038]. Their "dead tank" issue due to settlement delays makes them the highest-intent group for Moniepoint's instant settlement value proposition [S035, S039].

### 4. Market Opportunity and Missed-Revenue Audit

Excluding the following sectors creates a significant opportunity cost:

1.  **Petrol Station Networks:** Often overlooked by retail-centric ICPs, they have the most acute "liquidity-for-stock" pressure, making them a prime candidate for both payments and credit products [S035, S036].
2.  **Pharmacy Chains:** These share the same high-frequency, multi-location retail characteristics as supermarkets, with the added complexity of pharmaceutical regulatory requirements and high-value stock management [S040, S043].
3.  **Logistics/Haulage:** These businesses support the primary target (FMCG) and face similar cash-flow hurdles in fleet maintenance and driver expenses [S033, S034].

## 5. Anti-ICP, Not-a-Fit Criteria and Exclusion Logic

A business must satisfy the relevant segment's mandatory qualification conditions before entering the qualified-account pipeline.

Industry membership alone does not establish ICP fit.

| Not-a-fit category                                                                  | Exclusion reason                                                                                                              | Qualification decision                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Single-location microbusinesses without evidence of scaling                         | Insufficient evidence of multi-location financial complexity or significant transaction coordination requirements.            | Exclude unless other documented evidence establishes meaningful transaction volume and operational complexity. |
| Informal sole proprietors without demonstrable financial infrastructure             | No verified evidence of formal financial processes, digital payment infrastructure or scaling requirements.                   | Exclude unless additional evidence establishes a commercially relevant operating model.                        |
| Petrol stations operating only one location                                         | Does not meet the approved three-station minimum for the petrol station network segment.                                      | Exclude from `PETROL_STATION_NETWORK`.                                                                         |
| FMCG traders without significant warehousing or distribution operations             | Trading activity alone does not establish the depot, warehousing, receivables or distribution complexity required by the ICP. | Exclude unless mandatory distribution and operational conditions are verified.                                 |
| Retail businesses with no verifiable branch network                                 | A retail business is not automatically a retail chain.                                                                        | Exclude from `RETAIL_SUPERMARKET` unless the approved multi-location requirements are met.                     |
| Businesses with announced but unverified expansion                                  | Intended expansion does not establish an operationally active or committed change in business scale.                          | Retain for research; do not qualify on the announcement alone.                                                 |
| Dormant, inactive or permanently closed businesses                                  | No credible evidence of ongoing commercial operations.                                                                        | Exclude.                                                                                                       |
| Companies without a verifiable Nigerian operating entity                            | The project targets qualified Nigerian businesses and requires a verifiable operating entity.                                 | Exclude unless the Nigerian operating entity can be established.                                               |
| Public-sector infrastructure projects without a qualifying private operating entity | Public infrastructure investment does not automatically create a private-sector commercial prospect.                          | Exclude from private-sector targeting unless a qualifying commercial operator is independently verified.       |
| Businesses whose operations fall outside the approved ICP segments                  | Industry adjacency alone does not establish an approved segment fit.                                                          | Exclude from the current campaign or retain separately for future ICP research.                                |

### Exclusion safeguards

- Do not disqualify a business simply because a relevant finance contact has not yet been identified.
- Do not treat the absence of public payment-friction evidence as proof that the business has no payment problem.
- Do not qualify a business merely because it has recruited a finance professional.
- Do not treat an announced expansion as a completed expansion.
- Do not confuse a business's industry with its actual operating model.
- Do not infer Moniepoint product suitability or credit eligibility from ICP qualification alone.

**Decision principle:** An account must satisfy the approved ICP's segment-specific requirements. Buying signals and personas support prioritisation and research; neither can override an exclusion or substitute for mandatory qualification.
