# icp/signals.md

## 1. Research Scope and Methodology

This report provides a systematic identification of buying signals for Moniepoint based on the approved ICP. It evaluates operational and financial events within seven designated segments to align with Moniepoint's core value propositions: instant settlement, working capital credit, and multi-location financial management. Methodology includes analyzing 49 sources for triggers of financial complexity and operational expansion.

## 2. Approved ICP Segments and Priority Classifications

- **PRIMARY:** FMCG Distributors, Petrol Station Networks, Retail Chains (Supermarkets).
- **SECONDARY:** Quick-Service Restaurant (QSR) Groups, Pharmacy Chains.
- **EMERGING:** Logistics/Haulage, Specialized Healthcare/Diagnostic Networks.

## 3. Executive Summary

The research confirms that liquidity management (specifically the "dead tank" phenomenon in petrol stations and trade receivable delays in FMCG) is the primary driver for Moniepoint adoption. Buying signals are categorized by their alignment with Moniepoint's ability to solve T+1 settlement delays and provide data-backed working capital.

## 4. Segment-Specific Buying Signals

- **FMCG Distributors:** Recruitment for Finance Managers/Treasury roles [S014]; commissioning of new warehouses [S010]; entry into new regional distribution agreements [S009, S016].
- **Petrol Station Networks:** New station commissioning [S021, S023]; CNG conversion infrastructure investment [S033, S034]; adoption of non-fuel retail (convenience/QSR) [S022, S025].
- **Retail/Supermarkets:** Expansion into new geographic regions/branches [S098]; hiring of financial controllers for multi-outlet management [S089, S097].
- **QSR Groups:** Multi-outlet expansion [S008]; deployment of centralized POS management systems [S009].
- **Pharmacy Chains:** Acquisitions of independent drugstores [S040]; hiring for accounting/reconciliation roles [S043].
- **Logistics:** Fleet expansion [S064]; new haulage contracts for FMCG clients [S065].
- **Healthcare:** Expansion to new diagnostic centers [S050]; investment in digital billing/payment platforms [S053].

## 5. Cross-Segment Buying Signals

- **Finance/Treasury Recruitment:** A strong indicator of scaling complexity in all segments [S035, S036, S037].
- **Infrastructure Expansion:** New physical locations consistently correlate with increased reconciliation friction [S097].
- **Supplier Agreement Changes:** Indicates potential shifts in working capital requirements [S018].

## 6. Commercial Signal Categories

1.  **Financial Complexity:** Need for consolidated reporting across branches.
2.  **Operational Expansion:** New outlets creating "dead tank" or stockout risks.
3.  **Payment Infrastructure:** Transition from manual ledger/cash to digital/POS [S028].
4.  **Working-Capital Pressure:** Inability to fund stock due to T+1 settlements [S026].
5.  **Organizational Change:** New ownership or management restructuring [S036].

## 7. Signal Strength and Evidence Assessment

- **Strong:** Finance/Treasury recruitment (Directly linked to financial oversight needs) [S035].
- **Strong:** New Petrol Station/Warehouse openings (High correlation with T+1 settlement pain) [S021, S026].
- **Moderate:** CNG infrastructure investment (Indicates capital intensive growth) [S033].
- **Unvalidated Hypothesis:** Pharmacy churn rates or specific internal treasury maturity levels [S040].

## 8. Four-Test Buying Signal Evaluation Framework

Every buying signal must be evaluated against four commercial and operational tests before it is selected for automated detection or campaign prioritisation.

### 8.3 Interpretation of Individual Signals

**Finance/Treasury Recruitment**

A finance or treasury vacancy can reveal investment in financial oversight and an identifiable finance function.

However, the vacancy does not establish that the company intends to change banking providers or that the new employee has purchasing authority.

The signal becomes more useful when the advertised responsibilities relate to treasury, cash management, collections, reconciliation or multi-location financial control.

**Warehouse/Depot Commissioning**

A new warehouse or depot can increase the number of operating locations, financial workflows and coordination requirements.

Verify that the facility belongs to the target company and is operational or demonstrably commissioned.

**Geographic Distribution Expansion**

Expansion into new regions can create additional collection, supplier-payment and financial coordination requirements.

Verify actual operating expansion rather than relying on broad growth announcements.

**Supplier/Distribution Agreements**

New commercial agreements can change purchasing obligations, distribution responsibilities and trade-payment arrangements.

An agreement does not independently establish payment friction or increased credit demand.

**Petrol Station Commissioning**

New stations increase the potential number of payment-acceptance and reconciliation locations.

The signal becomes commercially stronger when the network has multiple operating stations and evidence of relevant transaction complexity.

**CNG/EV Investment**

Infrastructure investment can indicate business expansion and capital deployment.

It does not establish a payment problem, immediate liquidity shortage or eligibility for working-capital facilities.

**Non-Fuel Retail Diversification**

Convenience stores, restaurants and other station-based offerings can increase transaction types and operating complexity.

Investigate whether these activities are operated by the same commercial entity.

**Supermarket Branch Expansion**

New branches create a reason to investigate centralised financial reporting, collections and settlement coordination.

Verify the actual outlet opening and whether it belongs to the same operating company.

**QSR Multi-Outlet Expansion**

Additional restaurant locations can increase the complexity of outlet collections and consolidated financial reporting.

A single new outlet does not automatically establish significant financial complexity.

**Centralised POS/Payment-System Deployment**

A documented systems deployment creates an opportunity to investigate payment acceptance, settlement reporting and integration with finance.

Distinguish the software vendor's announcement from verified adoption by the target business.

**Pharmacy Acquisitions**

Acquisitions can introduce additional operating locations, supplier relationships and financial integration requirements.

Verify the acquiring entity and whether the acquired outlets remain under its operational control.

**Fleet Expansion**

Additional vehicles can increase operating expenditure, driver-payment coordination and fleet-related financial administration.

Fleet investment alone does not prove demand for credit.

**Haulage Contracts**

New contracts can change collection schedules, operating expenditure and payment obligations.

Verify the contract, the contracting entity and whether the company is the service provider or customer.

**Diagnostic-Centre Expansion**

New centres can create additional patient-collection, corporate-payment and billing-related reporting requirements.

Verify the private operating entity and distinguish commercial operators from public-sector projects.

**Billing/Payment Infrastructure Investment**

A documented investment in billing or payment infrastructure provides a reason to investigate how the organisation coordinates collections and financial reporting.

It does not automatically indicate dissatisfaction with existing banking arrangements.

**Payment Infrastructure Transition**

A verified change in payment acceptance or processing arrangements can indicate active financial infrastructure evaluation.

Establish what changed, when it changed and which entity made the decision.

**Explicit Settlement/Reconciliation Friction**

Public statements about settlement delays, reconciliation failures or payment difficulties can provide a particularly relevant commercial opening.

However, these statements are less frequently available and may not identify the affected company's current circumstances.

Do not transfer an industry-wide problem to an individual account without account-level evidence.

**Ownership/Management Change**

A change in ownership or leadership can create an opportunity to investigate financial governance and banking relationships.

Do not assume that new management intends to replace existing financial service providers.

### 8.4 Signal Selection Decision

Two signals receive priority for initial campaign implementation.

| Selection | Signal                   | Decision                                                        | Reason                                                                                                                                      |
| --------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PRIMARY   | `FINANCE_RECRUITMENT`    | Select for initial automated detection                          | Strong detectability and a credible conversation around financial oversight, despite moderate scores on buying urgency and pipeline volume. |
| SECONDARY | `MULTI_BRANCH_EXPANSION` | Select for secondary automated detection and account enrichment | Strong buying reason and credible commercial relevance, with moderate volume and detection reliability.                                     |

The selection is based on the combined usefulness of the four tests, not on an assumption that every detected account is ready to purchase.

### 8.5 Initial Automation Requirements

**FINANCE_RECRUITMENT**

Recommended detection terms:

- Finance Manager
- Financial Controller
- Treasury Manager
- Head of Finance
- Group Accountant
- Finance Operations Manager
- Treasury Lead

Verification requirements:

1. Confirm that the vacancy belongs to the target company.
2. Verify that the position is current.
3. Extract the responsibilities and seniority.
4. Determine whether the role appears newly created or replacement-based.
5. Establish whether the responsibilities relate to the ICP's financial workflows.
6. Record the source and detection date.

**MULTI_BRANCH_EXPANSION**

Recommended detection events:

- New branch openings.
- New petrol station commissioning.
- New warehouses or depots.
- Geographic distribution expansion.
- New diagnostic or pharmacy locations.
- Multi-outlet restaurant expansion.

Verification requirements:

1. Confirm the new location or expansion event.
2. Establish the operating company's identity.
3. Verify that the location is attributable to the target account.
4. Distinguish actual openings from proposed expansion.
5. Record the previous and current known location counts where available.
6. Record the source and detection date.

### 8.6 Signal Interpretation Safeguards

- A signal is not ICP qualification.
- A signal is not proof of purchase intent.
- A signal is not proof of a payment or settlement problem.
- A signal is not proof that the company wants to change banking providers.
- A signal is not proof of Moniepoint product or credit eligibility.
- Multiple signals increase research priority only when independently verified.
- Signal strength must remain separate from persona confidence and account qualification.
- Missing public evidence must not be represented as evidence that a problem does not exist.

### 8.7 Relationship to Personas and Workflow

`icp.md` establishes whether the company qualifies.

`signals.md` establishes what observable events justify further investigation and which signals merit automation.

`personas.md` establishes which buying roles and commercial angles should be researched.

`workflow.md` governs detection, verification, account routing, evidence capture, human review and outreach approval.

The resulting sequence is:

`ICP QUALIFICATION → SIGNAL DETECTION → SIGNAL SCORING → ACCOUNT ROUTING → PERSONA IDENTIFICATION → HUMAN REVIEW → OUTREACH APPROVAL`

## 9. High-Value Signal Combinations

- **Expansion + Recruitment:** A new branch opening combined with a finance role posting suggests a formalization of treasury operations.
- **Infrastructure + Credit Demand:** CNG/EV investment coupled with an expressed need for working capital indicates an ideal candidate for Moniepoint loans [S026].

## 10. False Positives and Qualification Safeguards

- **Routine Recruitment:** Replacing a departing accountant is not a growth signal.
- **Generic POS Adoption:** Some businesses use basic POS without the volume or reconciliation needs for Moniepoint’s advanced treasury tools.
- **Marketing Announcements:** News of "intended" expansion often exceeds actual capital deployment.

## 11. Practical Detection and Verification Framework

- **Automated:** Search for job titles (Finance Manager, Treasury Controller) at scale [S095]; monitor press for new branch openings [S021].
- **Manual:** Verify current payment provider via on-site observation or company partnership disclosures; check if the finance role is "replacing" or "creating" a position.

## 14. Final Buying Signals Recommendations

- **Best Signals:** Petrol station infrastructure investment and FMCG warehouse expansion.
- **Automated:** Finance recruitment; location growth announcements.
- **Manual:** Verification of existing payment settlement cycles (T+1 vs. Instant).
